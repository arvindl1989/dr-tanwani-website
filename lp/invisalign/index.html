Why a specialist

Aligners move teeth and roots. Planning matters.

Clear aligners are often presented as a product you order. What actually decides the result is the diagnosis behind them: which teeth move, in what order, and how the roots are positioned as they go. An MDS in Orthodontics is three years of training after dentistry in exactly that, and it is what separates a planned case from a set of trays.

Questions

Invisalign, answered

Does it hurt?
You feel pressure or tightness for a day or two with each new set, which settles quickly. There are no wires or brackets to rub against the cheek.
How long does it take?
It depends on the case. Minor corrections can finish in a few months; more complex cases usually run 12–18 months or longer. You get an estimate after the assessment.
Can I remove them to eat?
Yes. Take the aligners out for anything other than plain water, and brush before putting them back in.
How many hours a day do I wear them?
About 22. Teeth move along the planned path only with consistent wear, which is the single biggest factor in finishing on schedule.
Am I too old for aligners?
There is no upper age limit as long as your teeth and gums are healthy. That is checked at the consultation.
What if I had braces before?
Teeth commonly shift back in the years after treatment if retainers are stopped. Aligners are frequently used to correct that kind of relapse.
What happens after treatment?
You wear retainers to hold the result, usually full-time at first and then at night. Retention is part of the treatment, not an optional extra.
How much does it cost?
Typically ₹1 lakh to ₹2.5 lakh depending on complexity, confirmed after your assessment. EMI options are available.
