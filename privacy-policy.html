Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect the information you share with the clinic.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. Information submitted through our website, advertisements, lead forms or other communication channels may include your name, phone number and other information you voluntarily provide.

We use this information to respond to enquiries, schedule appointments, provide information about our dental services and communicate with you regarding your request.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Information may only be shared where necessary to provide our services or where required by law.

If you would like to access, correct or request deletion of your personal information, please contact Dr. Tanwani's Braces & Dental Clinic at drtanwanisdentalclinic@gmail.com or call 095296 63347.
