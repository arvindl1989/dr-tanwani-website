Parent's Guide

The Golden Age-7 Orthodontic Rule

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends every child receive an orthodontic evaluation by age 7.

01
Thumb Sucking

Prolonged sucking habits after age 4 can push upper teeth forward and alter natural jaw shape.

02
Mouth Breathing

Mouth breathing narrows the upper dental arch, causing severe future crowding and poor facial aesthetics.

03
Crowded Eruption

Lack of space for emerging adult teeth can be gently corrected with palate expanders.

04
Early Tooth Loss

Losing baby teeth prematurely causes neighboring teeth to drift, blocking adult teeth.
