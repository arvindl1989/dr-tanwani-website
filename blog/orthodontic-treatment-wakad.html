Orthodontic Treatment in Wakad: Your Guide to a Straighter, Healthier Smile

Written by Dr. Hemlata Tanwani, MDS (Orthodontics & Dentofacial Orthopaedics)
Specialist in Orthodontics & Dentofacial Orthopaedics · 15+ Years Clinical Practice

Crooked or crowded teeth aren't just about appearance. They can make brushing harder, put strain on your jaw, and affect how comfortably you bite and chew.

Orthodontics is the branch of dentistry that corrects these problems by gently moving teeth into their ideal position.

At Dr. Tanwani's Braces & Dental Clinic in Wakad, orthodontic care is our focus. Here's what you need to know if you're considering treatment for yourself or your child.

What does an orthodontist treat?

Orthodontic treatment can correct a wide range of dental alignment and bite problems:

Crowded teeth — not enough space in the jaw, so teeth overlap or twist
Gaps between teeth — spaces caused by missing teeth or teeth that are smaller than usual
Overbite — upper front teeth overlapping the lower teeth too much
Underbite — lower teeth sitting in front of the upper teeth
Crossbite — some upper teeth sitting inside the lower teeth when you bite
Open bite — front teeth don't meet when the back teeth are closed
Protruding teeth — front teeth that stick out, which also makes them easier to injure

If you notice any of these in yourself or your child, an orthodontic consultation is a good first step.

Signs you might need orthodontic treatment

  • Teeth that look crooked, crowded or widely spaced
  • Difficulty biting or chewing certain foods
  • Frequently biting your cheek or the roof of your mouth
  • Jaw discomfort, or clicking when you open and close your mouth
  • Food regularly getting trapped between teeth
  • Feeling self-conscious about your smile

Treatment options

Every smile is different, so treatment is always planned around your needs. The main options are:

Metal braces

Stainless-steel brackets and wires that steadily guide teeth into place. They're highly effective, even for complex cases, and are usually the most affordable option.

Ceramic braces

These work the same way as metal braces, but the brackets are tooth-coloured, so they blend in with your smile. They're a popular choice for teens and working professionals.

Clear aligners

Custom-made transparent trays that are almost invisible and removable for eating and brushing. They're well suited to mild to moderate cases in adults and older teens who wear them consistently. Read more about Invisalign and clear aligners.

During your consultation, we'll explain which options suit your teeth and why.

Orthodontics for children

An early orthodontic check-up around age 7 can spot developing problems with the jaw and bite before they become harder to correct. Not every child needs treatment at that age, but early guidance can make later treatment simpler, shorter, or sometimes unnecessary.

Our detailed parent's guide explains this in depth: Best Age for Braces: When Should Your Child See an Orthodontist?

Orthodontics for adults

It's never too late for a straighter smile. More and more adults are choosing orthodontic treatment, often with ceramic braces or clear aligners, which fit easily into professional and social life. Healthy teeth can be moved at any age.

What happens at your first visit?

1
Examination — a check of your teeth, bite and jaw
2
Records — photos, X-rays or scans as needed to plan treatment accurately
3
Discussion — your options, the expected timeline and costs, explained clearly
4
Your decision — no pressure. Take the time you need to choose what's right for you.

Benefits of orthodontic treatment

  • A straighter, more confident smile
  • Better bite and more comfortable chewing
  • Easier cleaning, which lowers the risk of cavities and gum disease
  • Less strain on your teeth and jaw joints over time

Life after braces: retainers

Once treatment is complete, you'll wear a retainer to keep your teeth in their new position. This step protects your results for the long term.
